About Joshua

I’ve been doing hair since 2007. I studied at the Aveda Institute in New York, learned to cut with Toni & Guy, and have worked behind the chair in New York City, Austin and Richmond, spending the last 13 years at 1213 Hair Studio. Nineteen years in, I still think a haircut is one of the most personal hours you can spend with someone. You trust them with your head, your energy, and with how you walk back out into the world.

CURRENT is for people who want to be listened to, not talked at. People who find salons a lot: too loud, too fast, too much. People in the middle of something, whether that’s a new job, a breakup, or a grown-out color they’re ready to let go of, who want a cut that fits who they’re becoming.

Cuts are priced by the hour, so you’re paying for the time you’re in my chair, not a guess. Color is booked in tiers. And if you’ve never had scalp therapy, start there. It’s a slow, quiet session with no small talk, just breath, touch and a full reset from the neck up.

I’m also a shamanic practitioner, so if you want a card pulled, a breath reset, or an energetic check-in tucked into your appointment, just ask. If you don’t, you’ll just get a really good haircut.

The space

CURRENT isn’t just a room I work in. It’s my offering to this community: the place where I show up for people, and where I keep my promise to steward nature and humanity toward right relation and balance.

It’s an inclusive, safe space, built with neurodivergent people in mind. I’m ADHD and autistic myself, so I know how much a room can ask of you. Here you can ask for silence. We can go easy on scent, soften the light, or change anything else that helps you settle. Just tell me what you need.

And the door is always open to conversation. CURRENT is about building community, one person at a time.

About Joshua

My work is about remembering. Not fixing you, not handing you a map, but helping you return to what you already know. Nineteen years behind the chair taught me how much people carry that they never say out loud. This is the room where it finally gets said, or felt, or set down.

I sit with people at thresholds of every kind: a loss, a move, a relationship ending, a new chapter that hasn’t found its shape yet, or the quieter moment when you just know something has to change. You don’t need to believe anything or have done this before. You just need to show up.

The work isn’t only one-on-one. I hold group journeys, and I’m the founder of The Caul, a men’s community here in Richmond, with a monthly Men’s Circle and Wild Enuff, a first-Friday walk in nature for men.

In 2025 I completed shamanic practitioner training under my mentor, Beáta. I trained in soul retrieval with her in Portugal, and I’m continuing that training in the Sandra Ingerman method. I’m working toward my breath facilitator certification in 2027, and I keep my own practice too, including solo overnight vigils. I don’t ask anyone to go somewhere I haven’t gone myself.

I work with drum, rattle and breath. Journeywork, soul retrieval, centering sessions, and breathwork coming soon. Anything to help you meet yourself and find the answers you seek in your own body.
